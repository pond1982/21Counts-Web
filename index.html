  • Running Count — hold the count across a full multi-deck shoe at table speed.
  • Basic Strategy — make the right play before the count even matters.

  • Casino Conditions — count through chatter, pit-boss heat, and busy tables.

Hi-Lo at a glance

Add +1 for every 2 through 6, −1 for every 10 through Ace, and 0 for 7s, 8s, and 9s. The running total — your running count — tracks how rich the remaining shoe is in high cards. A high count is your edge.
